DJ Dudes

Add songs to Queue A and Queue B to get started.

Plays your queues back to back, alternating A → B → A, fading each song into the next.

Automix starts fading 10s before the end of each song.

A B

Keyboard shortcuts

ZPlay / pause deck A
MPlay / pause deck B
XFade to deck A
NFade to deck B
SpaceFade to whichever deck is not live
← / →Nudge the crossfader
AToggle automix
Q / WBack / next on deck A
O / PBack / next on deck B

Drag songs from your computer onto a queue, drag songs between queues to move them, and double-click a song to load it on its deck now.
